Memories is a photo library for people who keep their photos on their own server instead of someone else's cloud.

Point it at your self-hosted Immich server and your whole library is on your iPhone: every photo and video, in a timeline you can pinch from years down to single days.

BROWSE
- Years, months and all photos, with a grid from 3 to 13 columns
- Albums, people, places and memories from past years
- Smart search: type "sunset" or "dog on the beach" and find it

BACK UP
- Photos and videos taken on this iPhone upload to your server in the background
- At home it talks to the server directly over Wi-Fi, so big videos skip the Internet

FREE UP SPACE
- Once a photo is confirmed on your server, Memories can remove it from the iPhone
- Anything still only on the phone is never touched
- Save to iPhone brings a photo back whenever you want it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account with us, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Your photos go to your server and nowhere else

Memories needs a server: it works with Immich, the open-source photo server you run at home or on a NAS.
