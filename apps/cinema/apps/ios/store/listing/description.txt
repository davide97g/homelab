Cinema plays the movies and TV shows on your own media server.

Point it at your self-hosted Jellyfin server and your library is on your iPhone and iPad: pick up where you left off, see what's next in a series, and browse every library you have.

WATCH
- Continue watching and Next Up, right on the home screen
- Native playback with subtitles and audio track selection
- Picture in Picture and AirPlay

YOUR LIBRARY
- Movies, shows, seasons and episodes with artwork and details
- Search across everything on the server
- Download episodes to watch offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- The app talks to your server and nowhere else

Cinema needs a server: it works with Jellyfin, the free open-source media server you run at home or on a NAS. It does not provide any content.
