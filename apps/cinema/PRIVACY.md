# Cinema privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Cinema for iOS is a client for a server you choose. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no SDK that collects data for anyone else.

## What goes where

- **Your server.** Everything the app shows comes from the server you sign in to, and everything it sends (your watch history and playback position, your sign-in) goes to that server only. If you run the server, the data is yours and stays with you.
- **The homelab account.** If you sign in with an email code, your email address is sent to accounts.davideghiotto.it, run by the developer, only to sign you in. It is not used for anything else and is not shared.
- **Your device.** Your session token is kept in the iOS Keychain. Settings stay on the device.

## What is not collected

No location, contacts, browsing history, identifiers, usage data or crash reports are sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Deleting your data

Data on your own server is deleted from that server. To delete a homelab account, open an issue at https://github.com/davide97g/homelab/issues or write to the developer, and it is removed with everything linked to it.
